Abstract
Robotic arm is a complex system with multiple inputs and outputs, strong nonlinearity and strong coupling, and the research
of high precision trajectory tracking control technology for robotic arm has been an important issue for scholars at home and
abroad. This paper takes the six-degree-of-freedom (6-DOF) robotic arm as its study object and designs a fractional-order
PID (FOPID) control method. To improve its control accuracy, a parameter tuning method of fractional-order beetle antennae
particle swarm algorithm (FBPA) optimized FOPID controller is proposed. This method puts the beetle antennae search
(BAS) algorithm together with the particle swarm optimization (PSO) algorithm, introduces the concept of fractional-order
calculus into the algorithm, dynamically adjusts the inertial weights and fractional order and ﬁnally improves the optimization
effect of the algorithm. The simulation experiments of MATLAB/Simulink indicate that in comparison with the traditional
PID control method, the FOPID control method optimized by the FBPA has high control accuracy and small overshooting,
which meets the high-precision control requirements of the 6-DOF robotic arm.
Keywords 6-DOF robotic arm · Trajectory tracking · Optimization algorithm · FOPID controller
1 Introduction
In the context of the new era of industrial revolution, the
application of robotic arms has been increasing signiﬁcantly,
due to their high intelligence, ability to perceive the external
environment and repeatable programming. Therefore, their
demand in medical care, military, industrial manufacturing,
etc., is increasing year by year [1, 2]. However, as a complex
mechanism system with multiple inputs and outputs, strong
coupling and strong nonlinearity, the robotic arm often has
uncertain factors during movement such as external inter-
ference and measurement errors. Its end position sometimes
completely deviates from the preset reference trajectory, and

When it comes to the domain of industrial robots, PID
control is a model-independent control method with a simple
controller structure and wide application [3]. However, it has
problems such as static error, slow response time and unsta-
ble output in tracking control. In addition, PID controllers
rely on the numerical model of the controlled system, but

or external interference in the control system, poor tuning
and poor performance may occur.
In order to break the limitations of PID controllers, the
academic community has widely adopted computational
intelligence technologies such as genetic algorithms, fuzzy
logic control and neural networks to control robots and
complex nonlinear systems. Hybrid methods to combine
PID controllers with other control methods have also been
adopted [4–6]. Recently, fractional-order calculus theory has
gradually been applied to controller design research. FOPID
controllers not only have some characteristics of traditional

The 6-DOF robotic arm is composed of six connecting
rods in series, which is suitable for working at any angle or
trajectory. Due to the rapid development of computer tech-
nology, the control of 6-DOF robotic arm needs more and
higher requirements. Today’s control methods are combined
with advanced control algorithms to better handle the 6-DOF
robotic arm’s own errors and external disturbances. Refer-
ence [9] used a new vision-based calibration framework to
maintain the accuracy of a 6-DOF robotic arm and achieved
good results. Reference [10] used the frequency response
function to improve the cutting stability of a 6-DOF machin-
ing robot. Reference [11] solved the movement problem of
a 6-DOF robotic arm in complex scenes by applying a deep
reinforcement learning method. Reference [12] used grey
wolf optimization algorithm to implement the optimal tra-
jectory generation method for a 6-DOF parallel robotic arm.
Reference [13] applied grey wolf algorithm to the inverse
kinematics analysis of a 6-DOF oil and gas pipeline welding
robotic arm and achieved good results.
This paper mainly studies the design and application of
FOPID based on FBPA, and the main contributions are as
follows: Firstly, the BAS algorithm is introduced based on
the PSO algorithm, with fractional calculus incorporated into
the velocity iteration update formula, leading to the pro-
posed FBPA. The results indicate that in comparison with
the traditional optimization methods, the FBPA improves the
convergence speed and accuracy to a great extent. Secondly,
in view of the complexity and uncertainty of FOPID param-
eter tuning, the FBPA is used to optimize and tune FOPID
parameters. Finally, trajectory tracking experiments are car-
ried out to verify the effectiveness of the FBPA and to prove
that FOPID has higher control accuracy with a 6-DOF robotic
arm.

2.2 PID controller and FOPID controller
Based on the feedback concept in traditional control theory,
a PID controller is designed to achieve effective control of
the system by adjusting three key parameters. The purpose
of the controller is to ensure that the output signal can accu-
rately follow the input signal [15]. PID controller’s structure
is shown in Fig. 1:
The input of the PID controller is the error e(t), which is
deﬁned as follows:

The transfer function of the FOPID controller is:

Compared with the traditional PID controller, there are
two more adjustable parameters for the FOPID controller,
the integral-order λ and the differential-order μ, and there-
fore have better control effect [16–18]. In addition, it shows
stronger adaptability, can reﬂect the characteristics of non-
linear dynamic systems more accurately and is suitable for
various types of systems. However, the additional parame-
ters will also make its parameter setting more complicated,
so the intelligent optimization algorithm is adopted to real-
ize the parameter setting of the FOPID controller. Figure 2
illustrates FOPID controller’s structure diagram.

3.2 Improved BAS algorithm
On the basis of the function of the beetle antennae and their
random walk mechanism, the BAS algorithm is proposed.
According to biological research, beetles locate its food by
the concentration of food odor; that is, they ﬁnd the global
optimal value based on the concentration difference of food
odor perceived by their two antennae [26–28]. The advantage
of the BAS algorithm is that the number of populations is
only 1 during the search process, which greatly reduces the
amount of calculation of the algorithm. The disadvantage is
that it falls into the local optimal situation easily [29, 30].
In order to enhance the algorithm’s optimization ability,
thisstudyputstheBASalgorithmtogetherwiththePSOalgo-
rithm and proposes a beetle antennae particle swarm hybrid
algorithm. In the beetle antennae particle swarm algorithm,
beetles are used to replace particles; likewise, the particle

4 Simulation experiment of robotic arm
In order to verify the control effect of the FOPID controller
based on the FBPA proposed in this paper, this paper selects
the UR5 6-DOF robotic arm model as the research object
and establishes a simulation model of robotic arm trajectory
tracking control in the Simulink environment [31]. The iner-
tia and partial physical parameters of the UR5 robotic arm are
shown in Table 2. The algorithm parameters in the simulation
are set as follows: The beetle population size is 30, the dimen-
sion is 5, the maximum number of iterations MaxIter=100,
the initial step size step=0.0001, the step size attenuation
factor eta = 0.95, the learning factor c1 = c2 = c3 = 2,
the minimum inertia weight wmin = 0.4, the maximum
inertia weight wmax = 0.9, the minimum particle velocity
vmin = −1, and the maximum particle velocity vmax = 1.

5 Conclusion
This paper conducts in-depth research and analysis of the
dynamics, FOPID controller and trajectory tracking control
of the robotic arm, which can be summarized as follows: The
dynamic modeling of the robotic arm is the basis for study-
ing the trajectory tracking control of the robotic arm. This

paper takes the 6-DOF robotic arm as the research object,
uses the Lagrange method for dynamic analysis and estab-
lishes the dynamic model of the 6-DOF robotic arm under the
premiseofensuringthedynamiccharacteristicsoftherobotic
arm. This study explores the basic theory of fractional-order
control and its system characteristics and designs a FOPID
controller. Through the simulation experiment of trajectory
tracking control of the robotic arm model in the Simulink
environment,theperformancedifferencebetweentheFOPID
and integer-order PID controllers is compared. According to
the experimental results, the FOPID controller can describe
and control the movement of the robotic arm more accu-
rately. At the same time, in view of the fact that traditional
BAS algorithm and PSO algorithm are prone to be trapped
intolocalconvergenceandprematureconvergence,thispaper
introduces the concept of fractional calculus into the algo-
rithm and proposes a fractional-order beetle antennae particle
swarm hybrid algorithm, which is applied to the parameter
tuningoftheFOPIDcontrollerandtrajectorytrackingcontrol
of the 6-DOF robotic arm. It greatly reduces the overshoot
during the operation of the 6-DOF robotic arm and improves
the response speed, obtains higher quality control effect and
veriﬁes its effectiveness and reliability in practical applica-
tions.
