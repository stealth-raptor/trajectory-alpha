Trajectory tracking control of a 6-DOF robotic arm based on improved
FOPID

Zhou Jiang1 · Xiaohua Zhang2 · Guoquan Liu3

Received: 29 September 2024 / Revised: 8 January 2025 / Accepted: 17 February 2025 / Published online: 31 March 2025
© The Author(s), under exclusive licence to Springer-Verlag GmbH Germany, part of Springer Nature 2025

Abstract
Robotic arm is a complex system with multiple inputs and outputs, strong nonlinearity and strong coupling, and the research
of high precision trajectory tracking control technology for robotic arm has been an important issue for scholars at home and
abroad. This paper takes the six-degree-of-freedom (6-DOF) robotic arm as its study object and designs a fractional-order
PID (FOPID) control method. To improve its control accuracy, a parameter tuning method of fractional-order beetle antennae
particle swarm algorithm (FBPA) optimized FOPID controller is proposed. This method puts the beetle antennae search
(BAS) algorithm together with the particle swarm optimization (PSO) algorithm, introduces the concept of fractional-order
calculus into the algorithm, dynamically adjusts the inertial weights and fractional order and finally improves the optimization
effect of the algorithm. The simulation experiments of MATLAB/Simulink indicate that in comparison with the traditional
PID control method, the FOPID control method optimized by the FBPA has high control accuracy and small overshooting,
which meets the high-precision control requirements of the 6-DOF robotic arm.

Keywords 6-DOF robotic arm · Trajectory tracking · Optimization algorithm · FOPID controller

                                           4 Simulation experiment of robotic arm

                                           In order to verify the control effect of the FOPID controller
                                           based on the FBPA proposed in this paper, this paper selects
                                           the UR5 6-DOF robotic arm model as the research object
                                           and establishes a simulation model of robotic arm trajectory
                                           tracking control in the Simulink environment [31]. The iner-
                                           tia and partial physical parameters of the UR5 robotic arm are
                                           shown in Table 2. The algorithm parameters in the simulation
                                           are set as follows: The beetle population size is 30, the dimen-
                                           sion is 5, the maximum number of iterations MaxIter=100,
                                           the initial step size step=0.0001, the step size attenuation
                                           factor eta = 0.95, the learning factor c1 = c2 = c3 = 2,
                                           the minimum inertia weight wmin = 0.4, the maximum
                                           inertia weight wmax = 0.9, the minimum particle velocity
                                           vmin = -1, and the maximum particle velocity vmax = 1.
                                           The input signal of the control system is a step input. The
                                           simulation experiments are carried out using three control
                                           schemes: PID control, FOPID control, and FOPID control
                                           tuned by the FBPA. The simulation results are compared to
                                           observe the tracking effect of the six joints of the robotic arm.
